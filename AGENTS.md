# AGENTS.md
Factorio 2.0 + Space Age (hard dep) colony mod. Design: `docs/factorio_colony_mod_design_document.md`. Work tracked in GitHub issues/milestones (M0 spikes+decisions gate M1-M4). Repo: alexswan10k/colony-bio-logistics. Owner uses macOS + Steam. API docs: https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/ (always verify 2.0 signatures here).

## Layout
`mod/` mod (Lua 5.2 runtime) · `mod/scripts/model/` PURE logic · `mod/scripts/spike/` dev-only API spikes · `tests/` · `sim/` balance · `scripts/` tooling · `docs/` (SPIKES, TESTING, DEPLOYMENT, ART) · `spike-results/` run reports.

## Commands
`scripts/test.sh [filter]` unit tests (luajit) · `scripts/sim.sh` balance tables · `scripts/lint-determinism.sh` · `scripts/link-dev.sh` symlink into Factorio mods · `scripts/package.sh [--release]` · `scripts/headless-spike.sh <spike[:args]>` · `scripts/save-load.sh` (#39 save/load round-trip) · `scripts/determinism.sh` (#39 two-run checksum) · `scripts/bench-colony.sh` (#39/#22 UPS gate) · `scripts/collect-results.sh` + `scripts/post-results.sh <report>` · `scripts/benchmark.sh <save>`.
Always run test.sh + lint-determinism.sh + `luajit -bl` on changed Lua before committing. Commit and push after changes.

## Rules
- Game rules go in `model/` with NO Factorio globals (`game/storage/script/defines/surface`); in-game code is a thin adapter. Test models offline; engine behaviour only via in-game spikes. Mocks encode beliefs, not evidence.
- Factorio 2.0 Lua API: Reference https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/ for exact classes, parameters, and signatures.
- Lua 5.2: no `|`, `&`, `//`, `goto` ok. No `math.random`/`os`/`io` outside spikes (use `game.create_random_generator`). Never store functions in `storage`. Register events via `scripts.events` at load time only. Schema changes need a `store.lua` migration.
- Do not edit vanilla prototypes (opt-in design pillar). Don't scan entities per tick; use registries + `scheduler`.
- Spike/decision outcomes: record on the GitHub issue (comment + close), update docs/design doc if the design changes. Track progress via `gh issue` and project milestones.

## Decisions made
Territory = chunk-granular 32x32 (#15). Space Age required (#12). Watchtower = 1-chunk ring, must be built on claimed land (#56). Farming = player-placed plots + tile fertility table (#44 first pass). Crates are plain containers (belts/inserters still work), driven by our own registry (#33). Construction = pay the ghost's `items_to_place_this` from colony stock, carry it, `revive`; repairs = one repair pack per 200 health (#37). Job locks are per-house, ore priorities are per-hut. Spoilage is cleared by colonists (free on a delivery trip, plus a lowest-priority haul band) into a Compost Bin that batches 10 spoilage -> 1 fertilizer (#78). Salt + preserved tier (#81): Salt Pan = worker-free, shore-gated by the *same* rule as the dock (1 salt/30 s, cap 8), emptied by haulers; Smokehouse = the first hut with **no tool** (gated by supply, not equipment: `labour.HUTS` omits `tool`, `hut_info` reports capacity, the job returns nil without salt + raw, inputs arrive on a `request`-priority haul offer, and `keep` stops haulers carting inputs back out); 1 raw + 1 salt -> 1 preserved with 3x shelf life (45 min) and the same nutrition, because #81 is about shelf life not throughput; preserved food is eaten last. Fishing Dock placement = six water tiles in a 15-tile box, sampled once at build time (`tile.collides_with("water_tile")`, scan outward, stop early), refused-and-refunded otherwise; fishers land 5 fish per trip with a rod that wears (#80). Hunting is real engine combat against docile game: boars live on their own `colony-wildlife` force (cease-fire both ways, so no aggro and no turret fire), hunters get `defines.command.attack`, and the colonist prototype carries a ranged hitscan attack with `vision_distance` 1 so nobody picks a fight unasked; a kill is credited to the hut from `on_entity_died`'s `ev.cause` (#79). Blueprint/copy-paste persistence rides on `BlueprintEntity.tags` (one namespaced key, dense row lists, `model/tags.lua`) because `storage` is invisible to a blueprint and `LuaEntity.tags` is ghost-only (#36). Housing tiers & water economy (#43): Homestead (Tier 2, 8 colonists, 3x3, needs: food, water 0.5/occ, heat via power or coal/wood), Apartment (Tier 3, 16 colonists, 4x4, needs: food, water, mandatory electricity, comfort furniture 0.1/occ). Fast-replaceable group "colony-housing" connects tent, cabin, homestead (cabin next_upgrade -> homestead), and apartment. Water economy: Colony Well (1x1, worker-free, 1 water/10 s, cap 12, emptied by haulers) and assembler water bottling (100 water fluid -> 10 colony-water). Mid-game food & kitchens (#44): Fertilizer is hauled to Farm Huts on request-priority haul offers (up to 6 stock, kept in hut); farmers spend 1 fertilizer per fallow plot planted (`plot.fertilized = true`), doubling harvest yield (2x: 16 produce at 1.0 fertility). Colony Kitchen (1x1, 2 workers, supply-gated like Smokehouse, no tool needed) bakes 1 colony produce + 1 colony water -> 1 colony bread (60 min shelf life). Bread is eaten last in housing.FOOD. Farm hut GUI reports plot breakdown (fallow/growing/ripe/fertilized) and fertilizer stock. Colony Science (#42, #19): Custom science packs (Frontier Charter, Civic Treatise) isolated from vanilla labs via engine whitelist (can_insert/insert == 0); perishable packs spoil into defines.inventory.lab_trash and haulers clear spoilage for composting; Town Archive (3x3, cap 2 scholars) and University (4x4, cap 4 scholars) are registered labour huts with job = "research" (M.RESEARCH_SHARE = 0.15, rank 2 default); scholars staff facilities while packs are present; native custom_status diode (green staffed / red unstaffed); technologies integrate into data.raw["technology"] and progress natively. Onboarding & feedback (#85): a new game shows only the Town Hall; five scripted-trigger milestone techs (`model/milestones.lua`: Settlement = build a hall, then 4/8/12/16 colonists in the best colony) unlock the early buildings, and Food Preservation gates the pack-based tree. The Town Hall workshop (`model/workshop.lua`) crafts a missing tool from stock for a waiting hut (1 per 10 s, unlocked recipes only). `scripts/feedback.lua` (every 300 ticks) sets `custom_status` on houses/huts/hall from `model/advice.lua` and posts native custom alerts for red problems (per-user `colony-alerts` setting); starvation kills an occupant of the starving house and names it; carried goods are drawn over colonists (`storage.carry_sprites`). Migrants still spawn at the hall: walking in from the territory edge stranded them behind water/cliffs. Motor Pool trucks (#60, #3): a car can only be driven by a character and has no autopilot, so the truck is a `unit` (`colony-truck`) that wears the vanilla car sprite and is pathed by the engine like a colonist (2.5x speed, 100-item load). A hauler whose haul is a full hand-load with more still wanted checks a car and the truck-sized load (up to 100) out of the nearest pool within 48 tiles that has a car and fuel, and claims both at checkout (a walker claims its load the same way). The driver walks to the pool and waits there under a stop order (`LuaEntity.active` is read-only in 2.0) while the truck delivers. Trips per fuel item are solid-fuel 6 / coal 2 / wood 1, and the pool burns the richest fuel it holds. The truck takes another big load while it has trips, a driver and daylight; it parks when its trips are spent, nothing big is left, or night falls. The car goes back to the pool, leftover trips stay in the pool's `tank` for the next dispatch, and a destroyed truck loses its car. Unlocked by the `colony-motor-pool` tech (30 frontier packs, needs Frontier Charter and vanilla Automobilism). Open decisions: #20, #21 (disease, tourism). #18 (housing) and #22 (perf budget) are decided and closed; the onboarding pass is tracked in #85 with an in-game checklist that feeds the #54 playtest.
